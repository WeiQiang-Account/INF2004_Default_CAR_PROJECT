Scan

Put the left sensor ~5 cm before the first bar. Distance must take the sensor fully past the last bar: barcode length + 10 cm (a 30 cm barcode needs 450 mm). If the scan stops on the barcode, the read cannot finish.

Connect, then press Scan.
  1. Robot in READY (IR calibration done). If it says FAULT, press Reset FAULT.
  2. Put it on the floor, pointing along the barcode, about 5 cm before it, with the barcode under the left sensor.
  3. Press Scan barcode: it drives straight over the barcode slowly, then back to where it started. Change the barcode or the speed and press Scan again.

What the barcode sensor sees (last 6 s)

Line = how dark the left sensor reads (0 = white, 100 = black). Shaded = counted as a bar. A good pass shows 15 shaded blocks (bars) with clear white between them. Thin bars that never reach high, or gaps that never drop, are the sensor blurring: lower it / point it straight down.
